Experienced and inexperienced riders will enjoy the horse ride. Our horses are carefully trained to give you a pleasant experience as you ride through the refuge — amidst the forest, on the beach, or in the river. Visit Playa Hermosa adjacent to Ballena Marine National Park, ride through the woods toward a waterfall and take a dip, or enjoy an astonishing sunset on horseback.

Tour Options
  • 🌲 Forest & River Ride — Daily at 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM
  • 🏖️ Beach Ride — Available based on tide schedule. Check availability when booking.
  • 🌅 Sunset Ride — Daily at 4:00 PM on dates when tide allows beach access.
Essential Gear
💧 Water 👖 Long Pants (mandatory) 👟 Closed-toe Shoes — no sandals or Crocs 🧴 Sunscreen 🦟 Insect Repellent
Before the tour
  • • No large bags or backpacks allowed.
  • • Avoid loose clothing, ponchos, or scarves — can frighten the horses.
  • • Arrive 20 minutes early for registration and instructions.
Safety
  • • Maximum weight: 230 lbs / 104 kg.
  • • No alcohol or drugs permitted.
  • • Inform us in advance of back problems, recent surgery, pregnancy, or severe fear of horses.
During the tour
  • • Guides may adjust pace or separate groups based on horse behavior.
  • • Phones and personal belongings are your own responsibility.
  • • Tour proceeds rain or shine except in extreme conditions.
Important
  • • Late arrivals may miss the tour without a refund — tour departs promptly.
